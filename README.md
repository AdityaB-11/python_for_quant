# Quantitative Finance Lecture Series

A comprehensive set of 56 lectures covering the foundations and advanced topics in Quantitative Finance.

## 🔹 Core Areas Covered

- **Python for Finance** – Basics of Python, NumPy, pandas, and plotting  
- **Statistics & Probability** – Means, variance, moments, correlation, random variables  
- **Regression & Estimation** – Linear regression, MLE, residual analysis, overfitting  
- **Hypothesis Testing** – Confidence intervals, p-values, and multiple comparisons  
- **Risk Management** – Leverage, position risk, VaR, CVaR, covariance estimation  
- **Portfolio Theory** – CAPM, factor models, PCA, optimization  
- **Trading Strategies** – Pairs trading, mean reversion, long-short equity  
- **Time Series Models** – Autocorrelation, AR, GARCH, cointegration  
- **Market Microstructure** – Volume, slippage, liquidity, impact models  
- **Advanced Techniques** – Kalman filters, futures trading, value factor case studies
